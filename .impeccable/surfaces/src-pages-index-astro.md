# Surface brief: portfolio home

Mode: Experience. One-page personal portfolio for people evaluating the author's work. The visitor scrolls through a single cinematic sequence; the work remains the evidence and the interface never invents biography or project facts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A portfolio that behaves like a dormant machine waking as the visitor scrolls, refusing the standard hero-plus-card-grid arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-black industrial ground, phosphor green and acid chartreuse signal light, oxidized steel lines, and authored SVG mechanical forms; typography is a condensed industrial display voice paired with a quiet readable sans.

STORY: The visitor enters a dark machine room, sees the author as its operator, advances through a controlled scroll sequence, and inspects one centered project at a time before reaching contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height black field with a restrained top rail, a large left-aligned identity statement, and a central rotating mechanical core already visible; the first project card is held in the center below the fold, previewed by the machine's green signal.

FORM: Scroll-driven mechanical observatory; seed: none (user-pinned direction supersedes the roll). Cards occupy the center of the viewport and reveal one at a time while SVG gears, pistons, and signal lines transform between chapters.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
